I want to rebuild a public dataset from its original sources, without
any starting files. Please do the following and show your work.

THE FIRST SOURCE. Stanford Open Policing Project, stops by the San
Francisco Police Department, release dated 2020-04-01. One zip, about
25 MB, at
https://stacks.stanford.edu/file/druid:yg821jf8611/yg821jf8611_ca_san_francisco_2020_04_01.csv.zip
No account or key is needed. It unzips to a 177 MB CSV of 905,070
stops, 2007 through 2016 — each row one stop, with the driver's race
as the officer perceived it, whether a search was conducted, whether
contraband was found, and whether an arrest was made.

THE SECOND SOURCE. U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey
5-year estimates, through the Census Data API — population
denominators for San Francisco County (state 06, county 075). Two
vintages tile the stop years exactly, 2007-2011 and 2012-2016:
  https://api.census.gov/data/2011/acs/acs5
  https://api.census.gov/data/2016/acs/acs5
This one DOES need a key. It is free and instant from
https://api.census.gov/data/key_signup.html — sign up, then attach
the key to every request. Keep the state and county codes as text:
"06" stops being California the moment it becomes 6.

WHAT TO BUILD.
1. One row per race group: stops, searches, contraband found,
   arrests. Then the two rates the comparison turns on — the share
   of stops that led to a search, and the share of searches that
   found contraband.
2. The same counts split by year, to see the pattern over time.
3. Population counts by race from both ACS vintages, averaged: total
   residents, adults 18 and over, and people who drive to work.
