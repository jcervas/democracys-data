Sources and method

Ages come from birthdates in the @unitedstates congress-legislators dataset (House and Senate, including non-voting delegates) and its companion executive file (presidents). Anyone who held a seat at any point during a year is counted in that year, aged as of July 1 — or, if their service that year ended earlier, as of their last day. Someone who switches chambers counts once in each.

Birthdates are missing for 542 mostly antebellum members; the year-by-year count of those omitted appears in the panels below when it is not zero. The last year with any missing birthdate is 1981. Six records whose birthdates are impossible (implying an age under 22 at swearing-in) were dropped. Median and mean are over members with known birthdates only.
