I want to rebuild a public dataset from its original sources, without any
starting files. Please do the following and show your work.

THE FIRST SOURCE. The U.S. Census Bureau's Data API, 2020 Decennial Census
P.L. 94-171 Redistricting Summary File:
https://api.census.gov/data/2020/dec/pl
Free, but it needs a key from https://api.census.gov/data/key_signup.html.
The variable for total population is P1_001N. The API will not return every
block in a state at once: a block query has to name a state AND a county, so
covering the country takes about 3,100 requests. Block GEOIDs are
15-character strings -- read them as text, never as numbers, or a 15-digit
identifier becomes 1.3121e+14 and leading zeros vanish.

THE SECOND SOURCE. TIGER/Line 2020 tabulation block shapes, one zipped
shapefile per state:
https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2020/TABBLOCK20/tl_2020_<FIPS>_tabblock20.zip
About 10 GB for the whole country. The attribute table carries GEOID20,
ALAND20 (land area, square meters) and AWATER20.

WHAT TO BUILD.
1. A table of every census block in the 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico with
   its 2020 population. Report the national total, and how many blocks
   recorded zero.
2. A count of blocks at each exact population from 0 to 1,000, with everything
   above 1,000 in one bucket. Report the median block population, and the
   median among blocks with at least one resident.
3. A per-state table: blocks, blocks with nobody, and the percentage.
4. A national map of the zero-population blocks. Join the zero GEOIDs to the
   TIGER shapes state by state, keep the matches, and dissolve touching
   blocks together. Report what fraction of the zero blocks have land area
   (the rest are water) and how many square miles the land ones cover.

THINGS THAT WILL BITE YOU.
- Check the join. Every GEOID from the API should match a TIGER shape. If it
  does not, you read an identifier as a number somewhere.
- Water blocks have zero population and are real. Decide whether they belong
  on your map and say which you chose; drawing them fills the Great Lakes.
- Dissolving makes polygons with holes. Draw each polygon's rings together
  under an even-odd fill rule, or the holes get painted as fill.
- Round coordinates only AFTER projecting. Rounding longitude and latitude
  rounds to whole degrees.

CHECK YOUR WORK. The copies this book used were downloaded in September
2026. If your rebuild matches them, you should find:
- 8,174,955 blocks holding 334,735,155 people, across 52 states and
  state-equivalents
- 2,368,443 blocks with nobody living on them -- 29.0%
- a median block population of 14, or 28 among blocks with at least one
  resident; 45.3% of blocks hold ten people or fewer
- emptiest Alaska at 58.82%, least empty Puerto Rico at 12.90%
- 2,130,094 of the empty blocks have land area, covering 1,465,387
  square miles -- about 41% of U.S. land, against 29% of blocks
- every one of the 2,368,443 API identifiers matches a TIGER shape; if
  yours does not, you read a GEOID as a number
Both sources are finished 2020 census products; they will not change
until the 2030 cycle publishes successors.
