The dataset behind this chapter cannot be rebuilt, even though its
source is open, keyless, and answering right now.

WHY NOT. The data are a snapshot of Polymarket, a real-money
prediction market: the highest-volume markets that had closed and
resolved to a definite yes or no, each with the price it traded at
7 and 30 days before it closed. The snapshot was drawn in August
2026 from Polymarket's public interfaces, starting at
https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/markets?closed=true&order=volumeNum
and following each market into the matching price-history service.
Ask the same question today and you get a different answer,
honestly: markets resolve every day, so the top of the volume
ranking is no longer the same list. A snapshot of a moving thing
cannot be drawn twice.

WHAT EXISTS INSTEAD. The same interfaces, today. They need no
account and no key — only a browser-style User-Agent header, since
bare requests are refused. A sample drawn now is just as valid as
this one; it is simply a different sample, and every calibration
question asked here can be asked of it.

WHAT YOU CAN STILL CHECK. These numbers identify the August 2026
snapshot this book uses:
- 746 resolved markets, each priced 7 and 30 days out
- the largest, "Will Donald Trump win the 2024 US Presidential
  Election?", with 1,531,479,285 dollars traded
- a Brier score of 0.064 for the 7-day prices — against 0.25 for
  always answering fifty-fifty
