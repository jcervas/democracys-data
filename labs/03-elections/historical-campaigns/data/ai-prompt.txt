I want to rebuild two public datasets from their original source,
without any starting files. Please do the following and show your
work.

THE SOURCE. Jason Timm's PresElectionResults collection on GitHub,
served raw from
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jaytimm/PresElectionResults/master/data/
Fetch two files: pres_by_state.rda, state-level presidential returns
from 1864 to 2024, and pres_results.rda, the national popular and
electoral vote by candidate back to 1824. Both are .rda files -- R's
own data format -- so read them in R, or with a library that opens R
data files. No account or key is needed. The state series is
compiled from public per-election records that themselves rest on
fifty-one separate official sources. Treat the deep past with more
caution than the recent past: nineteenth-century "popular vote"
totals rest on state records of wildly varying quality, and in 1864
eleven states were in armed rebellion and did not vote at all.

WHAT TO BUILD.
1. One row per state per election: the Democratic, Republican, and
   other shares, and who won the state.
2. One row per candidate per election nationally: electoral votes,
   the electoral-college total for that year, popular votes, and
   the popular-vote share.

CHECK YOUR WORK. The copies this book used were fetched in August
2026. If your rebuild matches them, you should find:
- 1,913 state rows spanning the 41 elections from 1864 to 2024
- 101 national candidate lines from 1824 to 2024
- for 2024: Trump 312 electoral votes of 538 and 77,302,580 popular
  votes, against 226 and 75,017,613 for Harris
The collection grows by one election every four years, and past
years can be touched up as records are reconciled; small drift means
a revision upstream, not an error in your work.
